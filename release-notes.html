Version 1.0.0

2026-11-07

Tiny Archipelago is ready for everyone, and we are so happy to share it with you. Take your time: there is nothing to miss and no reason to hurry.

  • Four floating islands, all yours. Restore them one by one, fill your workshop with machines, decorate the town and adopt friendly capybaras.
  • Neighbours who keep each other company. Put two seats beside a tea table, a campfire, a rug, a koi pond, the fountain or the music box, and two neighbours will sit down together and chat.
  • A Field Guide to fill. Eight kinds of fish bite at dawn, at sunset, in the rain and under the stars, and your guide grows as you meet them.
  • Little Steps, a gentle guide. Short chapters open as you unlock things, one friendly tip at a time, with nothing to finish.
  • The Coral Isle. One more island beyond the portal, with six tide pools where the sea comes in and goes out.
  • Your language. Tiny Archipelago speaks English, Spanish and German, following your phone’s language or the one you pick.

We hope it brings you many calm, happy moments. Tell us what you think!
